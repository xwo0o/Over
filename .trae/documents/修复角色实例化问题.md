### 1. 修改 CharacterSelectionController.cs
- 第21行：将 `characterIds` 数组从 `["Scout", "Architect", "Guardian"]` 改为 `["01", "02", "03", "04"]`

### 2. 修改 CharacterDatabase.cs（可选优化）
- 移除 CharacterData 类中不再使用的字段：name, description, attack, idleAnimation, specialAbility, specialValue
- 保留：id, health, speed, addressableKey
